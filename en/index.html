Effective date: November 21, 2026

Calisthenics at Home (קליסטניקס מהבית, calisthenics-home) is a Hebrew app for beginners: a graded calisthenics program you do at home, with no equipment. You can use it without an account. An account and cloud save are optional.

This is a fitness app, not a medical service. It makes no medical claims and no weight-loss promises. Consult a doctor before starting exercise.

You must be 13 or older to use the app.
