The service

Calisthenics at Home is a graded workout program for beginners, in Hebrew, for training at home with no equipment. It has four stages, workouts A and B, an exercise library with animations and instructions, a timer, and an optional weight log. An account and cloud save are optional.

The exercises use stable furniture at home, such as a wall, a chair, a counter, or a step. Some on-screen instructions tell you to check that the furniture is stable. You decide whether a movement is right for you.

Health and safety

The app does not diagnose, treat, or prevent a medical condition. It is not a medical device. It makes no medical claims and no weight-loss promises, and it does not promise a change in your body or a fitness result. The weight log shows numbers you typed and, if you typed a goal, the gap between that goal and your latest entry. The gap is not a plan and not a promise.

The plan screen says it is sensible to consult a family doctor before starting a new exercise program, that sharp pain in a joint (knee, shoulder, back) is a sign to stop, and that muscle soreness the next day can be normal in the first weeks. It also gives a rule of thumb: finish a set with about two repetitions left, and stop if your form breaks. Those are general reminders, not personal advice.

Consult a doctor before starting exercise. Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or any sign that worries you.
